📖 Help & Usage

How to use:

  1. Enable the location override toggle
  2. Enter your desired coordinates or use a preset
  3. Click "Apply Override" to activate
  4. Refresh the page to see the new location

Notes:

  • The override persists across page reloads
  • Works with all geolocation APIs (getCurrentPosition, watchPosition)
  • Use "Clear Override" to restore normal geolocation behavior
  • Changes take effect immediately on new geolocation requests
